# Cardbound

**Collect powerful cards. Discover unique abilities. Bring a little magic to your Minecraft adventures.**

Cardbound introduces collectible cards inspired by Minecraft's mobs, blocks, items, and environments. Each card features its own artwork, rarity, and unique ability that can help you explore, build, fight, and survive.

With **82 unique cards to discover** in the first collection, there's plenty to collect, experiment with, and master!

## 🃏 Collect & Discover

Open themed booster packs to discover new cards and expand your collection.

From common discoveries to legendary treasures, every pack offers a chance to find something special. Each card belongs to a themed set and features its own artwork, rarity, and ability.

You might even discover a **holographic variant** of your favorite card!

### Origins of the Realm

Begin your collection with **Origins of the Realm**, Cardbound's first card set, featuring 82 cards inspired by the world of Minecraft.

## ✨ Unleash Unique Abilities

Activate your cards to unleash abilities that complement your everyday Minecraft adventures.

Gain useful effects, summon helpful creatures, protect yourself from danger, or access convenient tools wherever you go.

Every card offers something different, encouraging you to experiment and discover which abilities suit your playstyle.

Custom sounds, magical particles, and visual effects make each activation feel special.

## 💧 Master Your Essence

Activating card abilities consumes **Essence**, a magical resource that gradually regenerates over time.

Your Essence is limited, so deciding when to use your abilities is part of the adventure. Save it for an emergency or spend it to make everyday tasks easier.

## 🌍 Made for Minecraft Survival

Cardbound is designed to **complement vanilla Minecraft, not replace it**.

There are no separate card battles, complicated decks, or entirely new combat systems to learn.

Your cards are simply another part of your adventure, ready to help you explore, build, survive, and discover.

Cardbound also supports multiplayer, so you can collect and use your cards while adventuring with friends.

## ⭐ Features

- **82 Unique Cards** - Discover the complete first collection, each with its own ability.
- **Origins of the Realm** - Explore Cardbound's first themed card set.
- **Booster Packs** - Open packs and discover new additions to your collection.
- **Multiple Rarities** - Find cards ranging from Common to Legendary, with distinctive rarity colors.
- **Holographic Variants** - Discover special versions of your favorite cards.
- **Unique Card Abilities** - Use magical effects, summons, utilities, and other powers during regular gameplay.
- **Essence & Cooldowns** - Manage your resources and decide when to activate your abilities.
- **Magical Activation Effects** - Enjoy custom sounds, particles, and visual feedback.
- **Customizable Essence HUD** - Choose different display styles and visibility preferences.
- **Animated Card Reveals** - Discover newly obtained cards through a dedicated reveal screen.
- **In-Game Settings** - Adjust HUD preferences, keybindings, card reveals, and activation screen flashes.
- **Multiplayer Support** - Collect and activate cards on Fabric multiplayer servers.
- **Survival-Focused Gameplay** - Enhance your Minecraft adventures without separate card battles or deck-building requirements.

## ⚙️ Customization

Choose between detailed and minimal Essence HUD styles, control when the HUD appears, customize your keybindings, and toggle automatic card reveals.

Settings are accessible through Cardbound's in-game settings screen, with optional Mod Menu integration.
